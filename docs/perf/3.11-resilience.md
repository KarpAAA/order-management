# 3.11 A provider that fails: what the retry and the breaker buy, and what they cost

payments-service calls its provider over HTTP. Since 3.11 that call is retried within the
delivery of the command and guarded by a circuit breaker (ADR 0020). This is what the two do
to the provider, to the service and to the client, measured against fake-psp. Decision and
rejected alternatives: ADR 0020.

Three gateways are compared everywhere below:

| Gateway           | What it is                                                              | Settings                                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `one call`        | a call per delivery, as before 3.11                                     | `PSP_MAX_RETRIES=0`, `PSP_BREAKER_MIN_CALLS=1000000` |
| `retry`           | up to two more calls within the delivery, after a pause with jitter     | `PSP_BREAKER_MIN_CALLS=1000000`                      |
| `retry + breaker` | and no call at all while more than half of the recent calls have failed | the defaults: what the service runs                  |

The defaults: a call 2 s, an operation with its retries 7 s, 2 retries with a pause of
200 ms to 2 s; the breaker opens above 50 % of at least 5 calls in 10 s, for 10 s, then lets
one call through.

One laptop, everything in Docker but the services; the numbers are one run each, and the
runs with a random provider differ from run to run by a few orders.

## A. The gateway alone

Reproduce: `pnpm db:explain:resilience` (`services/payments/prisma/explain/resilience.ts`),
after `pnpm infra:up`. It runs the real `HttpPaymentGateway` against fake-psp: 150 charges
per run, 10 a second for 15 s, at most 10 at once (`RABBITMQ_PREFETCH`). No broker: "failed
for now" is where the service would send the command to its wait queue. "Circuit open":
of the failed, those that ended on an open circuit. "Left waiting": arrived and never got
one of the 10 places before the run ended.

| Provider   | Gateway         | Charged | Failed for now | Circuit open | Left waiting | Calls at the PSP | p50 ms | p95 ms |
| ---------- | --------------- | ------: | -------------: | -----------: | -----------: | ---------------: | -----: | -----: |
| healthy    | one call        |     150 |              0 |            0 |            0 |              150 |     67 |     78 |
|            | retry           |     150 |              0 |            0 |            0 |              150 |     65 |     77 |
|            | retry + breaker |     150 |              0 |            0 |            0 |              150 |     67 |     77 |
| fails 10 % | one call        |     138 |             12 |            0 |            0 |              150 |     65 |     76 |
|            | retry           |     150 |              0 |            0 |            0 |              162 |     67 |    339 |
|            | retry + breaker |     150 |              0 |            0 |            0 |              158 |     64 |     78 |
| fails 50 % | one call        |      67 |             83 |            0 |            0 |              150 |     66 |     77 |
|            | retry           |     135 |             15 |            0 |            0 |              257 |     78 |    679 |
|            | retry + breaker |      48 |            102 |           96 |            0 |               94 |      1 |    587 |
| down (503) | one call        |       0 |            150 |            0 |            0 |              150 |     65 |     76 |
|            | retry           |       0 |            150 |            0 |            0 |              450 |    588 |    749 |
|            | retry + breaker |       0 |            150 |          150 |            0 |                6 |      0 |      1 |
| hangs      | one call        |       0 |             83 |            0 |           67 |               68 |   2006 |   2014 |
|            | retry           |       0 |             30 |            0 |          120 |               90 |   6415 |   6567 |
|            | retry + breaker |       0 |            150 |          150 |            0 |               27 |      0 |   2216 |

What the rows say:

- **A healthy provider pays nothing.** The same 150 calls, the same latency.
- **The retry is for a provider that hiccups.** At 10 % it turns 12 failed operations into
  none for 12 more calls; at 50 % it charges 135 of 150 where one call charges 67. A failed
  operation is three failed calls in a row: 0.5³ = 12.5 %, and 15 of 150 is what came out.
- **The retry alone makes an outage worse.** Against a provider that is down it makes three
  times the calls (450) for nothing, and against one that hangs it holds a place of the
  consumer for the whole budget: 6.4 s instead of 2 s, 30 operations handled of 150, 120
  left in the queue. This is the case the conventions warn about (`integrations.md` §3).
- **The breaker is for a provider that is down.** 6 calls instead of 450, every operation
  answered in under a millisecond, nothing left waiting. The 6: five to find out, and one
  trial when the circuit went half-open after 10 s.
- **At 50 % the breaker with its default threshold costs more than it saves.** It opens
  on a provider that still answers half of its calls, and for 10 s nothing is charged:
  48 charged against the 135 of the retry alone. More under B.

## B. What the client sees

The whole system (`pnpm dev`), fake-psp at `latencyMs: 200`. Orders of one item are created
and placed through the HTTP API, one every 500 ms, and each is polled once a second until it
leaves `PENDING_PAYMENT`. The time is from `place` to that moment. A healthy run: every
order `PAID` after about 3 s (the relays of three outboxes on the way, 1 s each at most).

40 orders, **the provider fails 50 % of its calls** (`failureRate: 0.5`):

| Gateway           | `PAID` | `PAYMENT_FAILED` `psp_unavailable` | Waited more than 5 s | Median | Longest | Calls at the PSP |
| ----------------- | -----: | ---------------------------------: | -------------------: | -----: | ------: | ---------------: |
| `one call`        |     36 |                                  4 |                   25 | 32.9 s |  94.4 s |               88 |
| `retry`           |     40 |                                  0 |                    8 |  3.1 s |  34.8 s |               81 |
| `retry + breaker` |     38 |                                  2 |                   35 | 92.9 s |  95.9 s |               79 |

20 orders, **the provider is down** (`failureRate: 1`):

| Gateway           | `PAID` | `PAYMENT_FAILED` `psp_unavailable` | Median | Calls at the PSP |
| ----------------- | -----: | ---------------------------------: | -----: | ---------------: |
| `one call`        |      0 |                                 20 | 94.1 s |               80 |
| `retry + breaker` |      0 |                                 20 | 92.4 s |               11 |

What the client sees is the same three things in every row: `PENDING_PAYMENT` while the
question is open (202 on `place`, then a `GET` that says so), then `PAID`, or
`PAYMENT_FAILED` with `failureReason = psp_unavailable` once the fourth delivery of the
command has ended, about 93 s later (three waits of 30 s). Nothing hangs and nothing is
lost in any of them; what changes is how many orders take the long way.

- **Before 3.11**, with half of the calls failing, every second order waited 30 s or more,
  and 4 of 40 failed (0.5⁴ ≈ 6 % expected) although the provider was there.
- **The retry** is what the client feels: all 40 paid, 32 of them at once, the other 8 one
  delivery later.
- **The breaker at its default made this case worse than it was before 3.11**: 35 of 40
  orders took the full 93 s. With half of the calls failing, "more than half of the last
  calls" is crossed every few seconds; the circuit stays open for 10 s; the commands it
  turned away come back together 30 s later, the one trial call fails as often as it
  passes, and the next 30 s begin. The log of that run: opened 5 times, closed twice.
- **With the provider down** the client sees no difference, and the provider gets 11 calls
  instead of 80: that is what the breaker is for.

## What to take from it

- A breaker does not make a failing provider answer. It decides when to stop asking, and
  the price of stopping is every call that would have passed. Its threshold says how bad
  a provider has to be before a call is not worth making, and with a retry in front of it
  that point is well above 50 %: three calls at 50 % pass 87.5 % of the time.
- The default (`PSP_BREAKER_THRESHOLD=0.5`) is the one the roadmap asks to look at
  ("fake-psp at 50 %: the breaker opens"). For a provider that is expected to limp rather
  than to fall, a higher threshold (0.8 and `PSP_BREAKER_MIN_CALLS=10`) keeps the breaker
  out of the way of the retry; it was not measured here.
- The two layers of retry are about different times: milliseconds in the process,
  30 s in the broker. The first hides a hiccup from the client; the second is the only one
  that survives an outage, a restart, and a failure that is not the provider's.
